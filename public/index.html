How it works

Three steps. Sixty seconds.

No app to install. No account. Just a chapter URL and a level.

STEP 01

Paste a chapter URL

Paste a link to any scrollable chapter page — Chinese or English — or upload your own pages. The whole chapter, page numbers and all.

STEP 02

Pick your HSK level

1 to 4. The text gets rewritten using only words within that level. Anything above gets replaced with something you know.

STEP 03

Read it rewritten

Tap any word for pinyin and meaning. Toggle back to the original any time. Save unknown words to flashcards in one tap.

FAQ

Common questions.

If something isn't covered here, drop a note on feedback. Answers go straight to me.

What manga sites does this work on? +

Any URL with scrollable manga. That's the only requirement. Most webtoon hosts work out of the box.

A few sites won't load, usually because they have download protections on their images. If yours doesn't work, send the URL via feedback and I'll see if it can be added.

Do I need an account? What happens to my chapters? +

No account needed — you can process and read chapters as a guest, and they're saved in your browser on that device.

Sign in (with Google or a code sent to your email) to keep your chapters in your account so you can read them on any device. When you sign in, we'll ask whether you want to add the chapters already saved in your browser.

Can I share this with my students? +

Yes. It's designed for exactly that. HSK Manga isn't a teaching tool; it's a reading tool for students to use on their own time, with content they actually care about. Share the link with your class and let them pick their own manga.
