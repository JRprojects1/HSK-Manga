HSK Manga

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

HSK Manga (“the Service”, “we”, “us”) is a free, small, independently run educational tool that rewrites manhua and webtoon dialogue into simpler Chinese for language learners. This page explains what information the Service handles and why. We have tried to keep both the Service and this policy minimal: you can use the Service anonymously without signing in, and we never collect a password. The only personal detail involved is an email address — and only if you choose to sign in to keep a saved chapter library.

Information we handle

Anonymous browser ID. On your first visit we store a random identifier (a UUID) in a cookie called browser_id. It is not linked to your name — it is used only for anonymous, per-browser usage analytics, such as a history of which chapters were submitted from that browser.

IP address. Your IP address is used to enforce usage limits (how many chapters can be submitted in a given time window), to limit abuse. IP-linked counts are stored on our server.

Chapter URLs you submit. When you submit a webtoon URL, we fetch the chapter images at that URL and process them. The URL and the resulting reader are stored on our server so the chapter can be re-opened and briefly cached.

Email address (only if you sign in). Signing in is optional and is used only to keep a saved chapter library. You can sign in with Google or with a one-time code emailed to you. When you sign in we store your email address, sign-in timestamps, and — if you use Google — your Google account identifier. We never store a password.

Saved library (only if you sign in). Chapters you save to your library (the chapter URL, title, HSK level, and when you added or last read it) are stored on our server, linked to your email, so you can reopen them from any device.

Server logs. Like most websites, our server keeps basic technical logs (timestamps, error messages, and similar) for debugging and reliability.

Anonymous usage events. To understand whether the Service is actually useful, the reader sends us small anonymous events: a chapter reader was opened, a word was tapped for its definition, the original/simplified view was toggled, a flashcard was saved. These events carry a random browser identifier (stored in your browser's local storage) so we can count distinct and returning readers — they are not linked to your account, email, or IP address, and we only ever publish them as aggregate counts (e.g. “readers this week”).

Information we do not collect

  • No names, phone numbers, or passwords. An email address is stored only if you sign in, as described above; there is never a password.
  • No signup or login is required to use the Service — signing in is optional and only needed for a saved library.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking. We use no third-party analytics and no advertising cookies — usage measurement is the first-party, anonymous kind described above — and we do not sell or share data with advertisers.

Cookies

The Service uses only a small number of strictly functional cookies:

  • browser_id — anonymous per-browser usage analytics.
  • session — keeps you signed in, if you choose to sign in.
  • oauth_state and oauth_return — short-lived cookies used only while you are signing in with Google.
  • Cloudflare, which protects the Service from bots and abuse, may set its own cookies.

Your theme preference and other local settings (saved flashcards, recent chapters) are kept in your browser's local storage. Your flashcard deck and local chapter list are never sent to us; apart from chapters you choose to save to a signed-in library, the anonymous usage events described above are the only reading activity we receive.

Third parties

To operate, the Service shares limited data with:

  • Cloudflare — bot protection and content delivery.
  • Google (Gemini) and Fireworks AI — the text extracted from the chapter images you submit is sent to these AI providers to be translated and simplified. Only the extracted dialogue text is sent.
  • Google — if you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google handles that sign-in and tells us your email address.
  • Resend — our email provider. When you sign in with an emailed code, your email address and a one-time verification code are sent through Resend to deliver that code.

We do not share your data with anyone for advertising or marketing.

Data retention

  • Processed chapters and their readers are kept temporarily and may be deleted at any time as server space is reclaimed.
  • If you sign in, your email address and saved library are kept for as long as the Service operates, so your library stays available.
  • Server logs are kept only as long as they are useful for debugging.

Your choices

  • You can clear your cookies at any time through your browser. Clearing the session cookie signs you out; your saved library stays on our server and reappears when you sign in again.
  • For anonymous use we hold nothing that identifies you, so there is usually nothing to link to a specific person. If you have signed in and want your email and saved library removed, email us.

Younger learners

HSK Manga is an educational tool, and learners of all ages are welcome to use it. It is a general-audience service and is not specifically directed at children under 13. Use of the Service without signing in is anonymous — it collects only an anonymous ID and an IP address, used to prevent abuse, and never a name or password. We encourage a parent or guardian to be involved in a younger child's use of the internet. The only step that involves an email address is the optional sign-in, which a parent or guardian should make or approve.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. The “last updated” date above will always reflect the current version.
